THE 4 PERSONAS

The four AI adoption personas in HR

The article groups HR professionals into four personas: Skeptical Avoider, Reluctant User, Active Explorer, and Adoption Champion. Each persona reflects a different level of sentiment, learning behavior, confidence, and AI usage. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

PERSONA 1

The Skeptical Avoider

Doubts AI’s value, avoids discussion, focuses strongly on risks, and prefers traditional methods.

  • Needs basic exposure
  • Needs safe, low-risk starting points
  • Needs reassurance and practical examples
Biggest risk: being left behind
PERSONA 2

The Reluctant User

Uses AI only when required, stays cautious, and avoids going beyond basic features.

  • Needs confidence
  • Needs guided use cases
  • Needs proof of practical value
Biggest need: hands-on confidence
PERSONA 3

The Active Explorer

Is curious, experiments actively, shares ideas, and wants more opportunity to apply AI in HR.

  • Needs structure
  • Needs broader HR use cases
  • Needs support to influence others
Biggest opportunity: become a catalyst
PERSONA 4

The Adoption Champion

Advocates for AI, leads conversations, promotes learning, and pushes for broader adoption.

  • Needs leadership-level capability
  • Needs governance and strategy
  • Needs tools to guide others
Biggest opportunity: drive transformation
