3 AI COACHING MODELS

Use the right coaching model for the right need

AI coaching is strongest when HR does not treat every situation the same. Different levels of complexity require different levels of human oversight.

MODEL 1

AI-supported coaching

Human coach first, AI in support. Use AI for reflection prompts, assessment support, and coaching preparation.

Best for higher-complexity and higher-risk contexts
MODEL 2

AI-augmented coaching

Human coach plus AI between sessions. Use AI to extend continuity, track focus areas, and guide action.

Best for scalable development with visible support
MODEL 3

AI-as-the-coach

AI provides the main coaching experience for narrower, lower-risk development needs.

Best where access and scale matter most
5 HR CHECKS

How to adopt AI coaching responsibly

AI coaching should be implemented as a deliberate people-development model, not as a random experiment.

CHECK 1

Define the purpose

Be clear on what coaching is supposed to improve and for whom.

CHECK 2

Assess complexity

Match the coaching model to the level of risk and human sensitivity involved.

CHECK 3

Set controls

Clarify awareness, escalation, privacy, and acceptable use.

CHECK 4

Validate the model

Make sure the coaching logic, design, and outputs can be explained clearly.

CHECK 5

Link to wider development

Position AI coaching inside your larger leadership and workforce strategy.
